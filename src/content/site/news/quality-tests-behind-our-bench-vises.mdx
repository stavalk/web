Quality is not a promise — it is a documented process. At Stavalk, every bench vise that leaves our 8,000 m² facility in Qingdao has passed a battery of laboratory tests designed to verify the performance we claim. This article walks through the key tests our QC team runs, the standards behind them, and why they protect both your brand and your end customers.

## Jaw hardness: 58–62 HRC

The jaws are the working face of any bench vise. If they are too soft, they deform or dent under clamping pressure; if too hard, they become brittle and may crack under impact. Our jaws are manufactured from 45# forged steel and hardened through induction heat treatment to a Rockwell hardness of 58–62 HRC. We verify hardness on a Rockwell tester on every production batch, sampling multiple points across each jaw surface to confirm uniformity.

## Clamping force verification

Clamping force is ultimately what a vise is bought for, and we measure it directly rather than relying on theory. Each model is tested on a calibrated hydraulic load cell to confirm that the maximum jaw opening and the acme-thread screw can deliver the rated clamping force. Ratings vary by model — our heavy-duty 8" and 10" units are verified at substantially higher forces than standard workshop models.

## Swivel base torque

A swivel base that locks poorly or drifts under load compromises accuracy and safety. We test the locking torque of the swivel base handle across the full 360° rotation range, verifying that the base holds its position when the vise is subjected to lateral loads during machining or filing operations. The anvil and base mounting points are also checked for squareness and flatness.

## 10,000+ cycle durability

Durability is verified through accelerated life testing. A representative sample of each model is cycled open-to-closed-to-open 10,000 times or more on a dedicated test rig. At the completion, we re-check jaw parallelism, screw backlash, and clamping force retention. Models that fail any parameter at any point in the cycle are rejected and the root cause is traced back to the production line.

## 48-hour salt spray corrosion resistance

Bench vises spend their working lives in workshops, garages, and outdoor sites where humidity and corrosion are constant threats. Our surface finish — powder coating on the main body plus zinc plating on the swivel base and screw components — is verified through 48-hour salt spray testing per ISO 9227. A passing result confirms that the coating protects the underlying HT200/HT250 cast iron and steel parts from rust.

## Certification and traceability

Beyond physical tests, every shipment is accompanied by full documentation: material certificates for cast iron and forged steel, heat treatment reports, dimensional inspection sheets, and test certificates for the mechanical tests above. CE marking is applied to models destined for the EU market, while ISO 9001 process controls govern how each test is performed and recorded.
