The Specialty Multi bench vise carries five integral stations on one rotating cast-iron body. Production environments, schools and workshops use it where consistent, quick **clamping force** is needed across multiple setups — flip the body and a fresh station is ready.

## Five workstations, one base

Each of the five stations carries hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC and indexes into a positive lock, so work stays rigid while you move between stations. It is a compact way to bring multiple clamping setups to a single bench position.

Every unit is verified for clamping force, swivel torque, jaw hardness and 10,000+ cycle durability, then salt-spray tested for 48 hours. Batch records are logged under full ERP traceability.
