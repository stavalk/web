The Medium-Duty 6" bench vise is the professional workhorse and our most popular model worldwide. Wide-body stability suits metalworking, woodworking and assembly benches, while the locking swivel base and quick-release mechanism let operators reposition work in seconds.

## Built for daily professional use

The body is cast in ductile iron for higher impact resistance than grey iron, then CNC-machined and finished with hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC. The quick-release mechanism allows rapid jaw travel without losing grip strength, and the swivel base locks firmly for repeat clamping.

## Measured performance

Each unit is tested for **clamping force**, jaw hardness, swivel torque and 10,000+ cycle durability, followed by a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Batch test logs ship with the batch documentation under full ERP traceability.

## OEM options

Jaw width, base type, finish and engraving are adaptable — a strong entry point for professional brands building a full bench-vise range.
