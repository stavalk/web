The Household Vise is our best-selling bench vise — a ductile-iron all-rounder for home, trade and light industrial use. Front and back pivot-and-lock positioning lets operators set the angle and lock it firmly, while replaceable hardened jaws and a flat anvil surface cover clamping, flattening and shaping in one compact tool.

## Construction and materials

The body is cast in ductile iron for higher impact resistance than grey iron, then CNC-machined and finished with hardened replaceable jaws at 58–62 HRC. The front and back pivot-and-lock mechanism holds the work at the chosen angle, and the integrated anvil provides a solid surface for flattening and light striking.

## Tested like every Stavalk vise

Each unit is tested for **clamping force**, jaw hardness, pivot-lock torque and 10,000+ cycle durability, followed by a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Batch test logs ship with the batch documentation under full ERP traceability.

## OEM options

Jaw width, base type, finish and engraving are adaptable — a strong entry point for professional brands building a full bench-vise range.
