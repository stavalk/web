The Lightweight Fixed Anvil Vise is an entry-level cast-iron bench vise built for hobbyists, students and home workstations. Its light body is easy to mount on any bench, while the fixed anvil and hardened serrated jaws deliver the precision needed for everyday bench work.

## Construction and materials

The body is cast in lightweight grey cast iron (HT200) and machined on our CNC lines for a precise fit. Hardened serrated jaws at 58–62 HRC press against the workpiece with a smooth ACME screw, and a fixed anvil provides a flat forming surface for light metalwork.

## Tested like every Stavalk vise

Each unit is checked for clamping force, jaw hardness and 10,000+ cycle durability, then finished with a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Batch test logs are kept with full ERP traceability and shipped with the batch documentation.
