The Heavy-Duty 6" forged bench vise is built for demanding work where rigidity and repeatability decide the result. Forging and a thick cross-section let the body sustain **clamping force** of 2,000–5,000 lb without flexing, making it the choice for metalworking, fabrication and industrial maintenance.

## Forged for serious grip

The body is forged from 45# steel, heat-treated and CNC-machined, then paired with jaws hardened up to 62 HRC and a replaceable screw assembly. The design delivers the impact resistance and tensile strength that heavy operators need, day after day.

## Measured performance

Each unit is verified for clamping force, jaw hardness, swivel torque and 10,000+ cycle durability, then salt-spray tested for 48 hours. Batch test logs are kept with full ERP traceability and shipped with the batch documentation.
