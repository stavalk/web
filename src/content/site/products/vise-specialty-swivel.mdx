The ADI Industrial Swivel Vise sits on a full 360° swivel base, giving operators complete freedom to position the work at any angle. Ideal for angled clamping, assembly benches and any operator who repositions work frequently in a production environment.

## Built for production

The ADI cast-steel body is impact-tough and wear-resistant, carrying hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC. The 360° swivel base indexes with a positive lock, so the vise stays rigid under clamping force while giving operators full freedom to rotate the work. Production-oriented build with a reinforced throat for high-cycle use.

## Measured performance

Each unit is verified for clamping force, swivel torque, jaw hardness and 10,000+ cycle durability, then completed with a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Records are logged per batch under ERP traceability.
