The Specialty Swivel bench vise sits on a full 360° swivel base with a spring-loaded release, giving operators complete freedom to position the work. Ideal for angled clamping, assembly benches and any operator who repositions work frequently.

## Built for repositioning

The cast-iron body carries hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC, while the spring-loaded swivel base rotates the vise smoothly into any working angle and locks in place. Combined with quick-release travel, operators can flip, rotate and re-clamp work in seconds without losing grip quality or **clamping force**.

Each unit is verified for clamping force, swivel torque, jaw hardness and 10,000+ cycle durability, then completed with a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Records are logged per batch under ERP traceability.
