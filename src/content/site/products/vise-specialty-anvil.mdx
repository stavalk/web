The Anvil is a cast companion bench tool with a hammer-rated striking face — a solid surface for flattening, peening and shaping metal right next to your vise. It fills the gap between a bench clamp and a dedicated forge setup.

## Construction and materials

The body is cast iron with a hardened flat face and a rounded horn for shaping. Its compact footprint lets it sit alongside a vise on a standard workbench, giving operators an impact surface without leaving the bench position.

## Tested like every Stavalk vise

Each unit is verified for surface hardness and structural integrity, then finished with a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Records are logged per batch under ERP traceability.
