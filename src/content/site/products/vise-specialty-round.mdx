The Movable Round Anvil Table Vise sits on a round swivel base with a movable round anvil, giving operators a compact and precise workholding solution. Ideal for finishing shops, schools and small workstations that need to reposition work frequently.

## Construction and materials

The ductile-iron body carries hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC and a movable round anvil for angled holding. The round swivel base indexes with a positive lock, so the vise stays rigid under clamping force while allowing operators to swing the work surface into the best position.
