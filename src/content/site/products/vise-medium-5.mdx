The Fixed Table Vise is a compact ductile-iron bench vise with a fixed base for finishing shops, schools and workstations that need a solid grip without the footprint of a full-size vise. Hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC deliver reliable clamping on small to medium workpieces, and the fixed base bolts straight to the bench for repeatable, stable positioning.

## Construction and materials

The body is cast in ductile iron for higher impact resistance than grey iron, then CNC-machined and finished with hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC. The fixed base eliminates swivel play, giving operators a rigid, stable platform for finishing, assembly and light fabrication tasks in space-limited environments.

## Tested like every Stavalk vise

Each unit is tested for **clamping force**, jaw hardness and 10,000+ cycle durability, followed by a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Batch test logs ship with the batch documentation under full ERP traceability.

## OEM options

Jaw width, finish and engraving are adaptable — a strong entry point for hardware chains, educational suppliers and brands building a compact vise range.
