The Heavy Duty Movable Anvil Vise is an 8-inch heavy-duty bench vise with a movable anvil plate and a reinforced cast-iron body — built for pipe fitting, welding tables and heavy fabrication benches. A reinforced HT250 cast-iron body and generous jaw travel generate **clamping force** of 2,000–5,000 lb, while the movable anvil plate positions the workpiece at the optimal angle for filing, fitting and impact work.
