The 6" pipe bench vise handles heavy pipe, larger diameters and high-torque applications. Its reinforced ductile-iron base and replaceable V-jaws are built for repetitive trade use, giving welders and fitters dependable **clamping force** on the largest workpieces.

## Heavy-duty pipe workholding

The reinforced base resists twisting under high torque, while the hardened screw assembly delivers solid clamping force through the full jaw travel. Replaceable V-jaws grip pipe, rod and square stock firmly without scoring the surface, and the anvil adds a forming surface to the bench.
