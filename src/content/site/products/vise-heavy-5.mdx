The Heavy Duty Fixed Anvil Vise is a heavy bench vise with a fixed anvil cast into the body — a stable, hammer-rated surface with full clamping force for commercial and workshop use. The integral anvil provides a solid striking platform while the hardened jaws and rigid body deliver the clamping performance demanded by trade and industrial environments.

## Construction and materials

The body is a heavy-duty cast iron casting with a fixed anvil integral to the structure, eliminating movement under impact. Hardened serrated jaws and a hardened screw assembly provide durable, repeatable clamping over years of daily use.

Each unit is verified for clamping force, jaw hardness and 10,000+ cycle durability, then completed with a 48-hour salt-spray corrosion test. Records are logged per batch under full ERP traceability.
