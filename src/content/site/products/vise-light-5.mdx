The Light-Duty 5" bench vise is the wide-body classic for garages, craft tables and general DIY. The larger body and full-width jaws give it a more confident grip than a 4-inch model, while the built-in anvil and integrated pipe jaws keep it useful across every everyday bench task.

## Body and geometry

Cast in grey cast iron (HT200) with anti-distortion geometry, the body is CNC-machined for a precise slide fit. Full-width hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC deliver even pressure along the entire clamping face, and the built-in anvil gives a solid forming surface for light metalwork.

## Measured performance

Every unit is verified for **clamping force** consistency, swivel torque and 10,000+ cycle durability, then salt-spray tested for 48 hours. Records are logged per batch and included in the batch documentation.
