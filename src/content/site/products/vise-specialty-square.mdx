The Square Anvil Table Vise combines a compact bench vise with a square-anvil top and a full 360° swivel base — a versatile all-rounder for assembly, maintenance and positioning work at home or in the shop.

## Construction and materials

The ductile-iron body carries hardened jaws at 58–62 HRC and a square-anvil top for light striking work. The 360° swivel base indexes with a positive lock, so the vise stays rigid under clamping force while giving operators full freedom to position the work at any angle.
