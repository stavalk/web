The Medium-Duty 8" bench vise brings big capacity and precise control to machining, workshop and maintenance benches. Its wide casting and double guide bars keep the moving jaw parallel even under heavy **clamping force**, so large workpieces are held evenly and securely.

## Design for large workpieces

Cast in HT250 grey cast iron with reinforced ribs, the body resists racking under load. Double guide bars keep the front jaw tracking straight, and the locking swivel base plus anvil plate add versatility across machining and maintenance tasks. Drop-in jaw caps are field-replaceable, so service life is extended without replacing the body.
